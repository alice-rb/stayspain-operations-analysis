# 🏠 Listing Characteristics & Availability

## 🎯 Objective

To understand how listing characteristics (capacity, price, and configuration) impact availability and identify the main drivers of inventory performance.

---

## ❓ Key Question

How do listing features affect availability, and do these effects differ across cities?

---

## 📊 Approach

- Analysis of key variables: capacity, price per guest, bathrooms, beds
- Regression models (OLS) to measure impact
- Logistic regression to validate drivers of high availability (low occupancy)
- Segmentation by city

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Capacity is a key driver: larger listings tend to have higher availability
- Price has a strong impact: higher prices are associated with lower occupancy
- Configuration variables (beds, bathrooms) have limited influence
- The effect of these variables is consistent across cities, with some variation

---

## 💡 Business Insight

Not all listing attributes are equally relevant:
- Price and capacity drive performance
- Over-optimizing configuration has limited impact
- Many listings may be misaligned with demand in terms of size and pricing

---

## 🚀 Implications

- Prioritize pricing strategies over configuration adjustments
- Re-evaluate positioning of large-capacity listings
- Avoid over-investing in low-impact features
- Focus on high-impact levers to improve occupancy and reduce availability
