# ⚡ Instant Booking Impact Analysis

## 🎯 Objective

To evaluate the impact of Instant Booking on occupancy and understand how its effect varies across cities and listing types.

---

## ❓ Key Question

What is the impact of enabling Instant Booking on occupancy levels across different markets?

---

## 📊 Approach

- Comparison of occupancy between listings with and without Instant Booking
- Segmentation by city and listing type
- Validation using regression analysis to isolate the effect

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Listings with Instant Booking show higher average occupancy
- The overall impact is positive, but not uniform across cities
- Some segments benefit significantly, while others show neutral or even negative effects
- Listing type does not significantly alter the general trend

---

## 💡 Business Insight

Instant Booking is not universally effective:
- Its impact depends on the market and segment
- A global activation strategy leads to inefficiencies

---

## 🚀 Implications

- Apply Instant Booking selectively based on city and segment performance
- Prioritize activation in high-impact markets
- Avoid activation in segments with negative or neutral impact
- Use platform-driven features as a lever for inventory optimization
