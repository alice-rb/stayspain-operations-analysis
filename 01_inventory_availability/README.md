# 📊 Inventory Availability Analysis

## 🎯 Objective

To analyze how availability evolves across cities and time horizons (30, 60, 90, 365 days) and identify differences in demand pressure.

---

## ❓ Key Question

How does average availability vary across cities and time windows?

---

## 📊 Approach

- Aggregation of availability metrics by city
- Comparison across time horizons
- Identification of patterns in booking behavior

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Availability increases as the time horizon expands
- Major cities (Madrid, Barcelona) show lower availability → higher demand pressure
- Smaller markets (Girona, Menorca) maintain higher availability → lower demand

---

## 💡 Business Insight

Markets behave differently:
- Urban markets → high demand, lower availability
- Leisure markets → more inventory available

---

## 🚀 Implications

- Prioritize optimization strategies in high-availability markets
- Adjust pricing and promotions based on demand pressure
